Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

The Laguna Tourism, Culture, Arts and Trade Office (LTCATO) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use this platform, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules and regulations.

1. Information we collect

When you create a tourist account, we collect:

  • Your name (first, middle, and last name)
  • Your email address
  • Your account password (stored in encrypted/hashed form)
  • Activity on the platform, such as saved spots, itineraries, and reviews you submit

2. How we use your information

We use your information to create and manage your account, verify your identity, personalize your experience (such as saved destinations and itineraries), respond to your inquiries, and improve our tourism programs and services.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with other offices of the Provincial Government of Laguna or partner local government units for purposes directly related to tourism services, or when required by law.

4. Data security

We implement reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erase or block, to data portability, and to file a complaint. To exercise these rights or request the deletion of your account, please contact us using the details on our About page.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Material changes will be posted on this page.
